## 2. Motifs (35) for simple vector drawing

1. Rain streaks and clouds: diagonal lines over the scene; sudden-shower humour (Bengaluru rain/waterlogging is well covered by local news, e.g. https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/sudden-deluge-of-rains-trigger-traffic-jams-water-logging-in-bengaluru-3221194).
2. Umbrella (black or leaf-green): the character holds umbrella in one hand, coffee in the other.
3. Raincoat / plastic poncho: for the commuter personality.
4. Puddle with ripples: reflection of the cup; also the "pothole full of water".
5. Pothole: small dark ellipse on the road; joke caption "Pothole count: 3".
6. Traffic policeman: whistle, white-and-khaki outfit, raised palm; stops the character to "check your coffee".
7. Traffic cone and barricade: border or divider on the result card.
8. Flyover: grey arch silhouette behind the character; the "elevated" mood.
9. Traffic signal: three dots; red/yellow/green as a tiny 3-step mood meter.
10. Namma Metro purple line: purple viaduct and a train silhouette crossing the background (purple and green line colours confirmed on https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namma_Metro).
11. Metro spiral logo idea: rangoli-spiral of dots as a decorative divider (do not use the actual logo).
12. Autorickshaw: yellow-and-black (Bengaluru autos are commonly yellow/black, model knowledge) with the coffee on the seat.
13. BMTC bus: simple rounded rectangle behind a character, for the budget-cafe personality.
14. Vidhana Soudha: symmetrical domed building outline; use for the "classic" personality.
15. Filter-coffee steel tumbler and davara: the hero cup shape for the guy/girl to hold.
16. Dabara-set steam: curly steam lines forming a Kannada letter or a heart.
17. Coffee beans / coffee estate leaf (Coorg and Chikmagalur origin): small repeating pattern.
18. Cubbon Park trees: round canopies and bamboo clump as the calm backdrop.
19. Jacaranda blooms: purple-lilac petals falling (Feb-Mar bloom per https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/karnataka/history-of-bengaluru-s-blooms-1092728.html).
20. Gulmohar blooms: orange-red petals (Apr-Jul bloom), pairs with yellow for a summer scene.
21. Tabebuia / pink trumpet trees: rose petals, matches the existing rose accent.
22. Sampige (champak) flower: a single flower in the hair or on the cup (Malleshwaram mural theme).
23. Marigold garland: border or hanging garland (mural "Svagata").
24. Tech-park laptop and ID lanyard: character with laptop sticker on a cafe table.
25. Headphones and "WFH" laptop stickers: remote-worker personality.
26. Dosa and idli plate: tiny side plate with a coffee (Darshini culture).
27. Masala dosa as a rolled cone, with chutney cups.
28. Biscuit / rusk / Maddur vada beside the cup (model knowledge).
29. Kannada lettering and signage: "ಕಾಫಿ" (kaapi) on a hand-painted board; keep short and verify spelling with a Kannada speaker.
30. Namma Bengaluru Habba-style festival art: stilt walkers, dollu kunitha drum, face painters (https://newsable.asianetnews.com/life/5-fairs-festivals-you-should-never-miss-in-bengaluru-pkwvb9 and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karnataka_Chitrakala_Parishath).
31. Chitra Santhe art-market stalls: easels and hanging paintings behind the pair (https://utsav.gov.in/view-event/chitra-santhe).
32. Channapatna toys: lacquered round-bodied toys in red, yellow, green as table props (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Channapatna_toys).
33. Kinnal toy / Kinnal craft: not verified by my searches (no result tied it to a source); treat as optional and check before use. Use a simple painted wooden figure silhouette if included.
34. Mysore silk: saree or dupatta in green/orange/red with a gold border as clothing for the girl character (colours per https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/karnataka/mysuru/shopping-of-sweets-sandalwood-and-silk).
35. Rangoli (kolam): dot-and-line pattern as a floor pattern or card divider.
36. Mysore-style gold-and-red border (Mysore painting): framing around the result card.
37. Coconut palms: from the Malleshwaram wall backdrop.
38. Stray dog / street cow / crow on a wall: small comic sidekick (model knowledge).
39. Cafe chalkboard menu and Edison bulb: indoor scene.
40. Auto-rickshaw "Namma" slogan sticker, in truck-art lettering style.

## 3. Colours and typography

Observed (from sources): Namma Metro uses purple and green; city logo uses red and black; Channapatna lacquer is red, yellow, green (natural dyes: lac, turmeric); Mysore silk traditionally green, orange, red, now also coffee brown and lilac; Indian truck/auto art is saturated yellow, orange, red, emerald, turquoise; Blue Tokai uses blue with earthy secondaries plus green and orange roast coding; Bengaluru coffee packaging agencies cite "vibrant like filter coffee or earthy like Cubbon Park" (https://palsa.co/blog/packaging-design-tips-bangalore/).

Suggested accent additions (hex are suggestions; each sits beside the existing palette):

|---|---|---|

| Jacaranda lilac | #b9a3d6 | Jacaranda bloom |

| Silk gold | #c99a3b | Zari border |
| Sky wash | #cfe0e4 | Rainy-sky background tint |

Rules: keep the base paper/cream/coffee; use one of the accents per scene; contrast check any text on a colour before shipping.

Typography: keep Fraunces (headings) and DM Sans (body). For Kannada text use Noto Sans Kannada (OFL 1.1, free for web embedding: https://fontsource.org/fonts/noto-sans-kannada). Treat hand-painted Kannada as image/SVG sticker, not live text. For a "signboard" display feel, consider an OFL display face from Google Fonts (e.g. Rozha One or Bungee, model knowledge; verify licence on fonts.google.com before use).
